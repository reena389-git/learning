00The two planes

Governance is a property of the distribution path, not the data. The same fact can travel a governed or an ungoverned plane.

GOVERNED Unity Catalog · CDO CDM · lineage · contracts · data products

Tables/views in Unity Catalog with captured column-lineage, assignable ownership, declared semantics (grain, unit, currency, FX source), and ODCS contracts. Where we are building.

UNGOVERNED Veritas + contractless app services

Veritas — incumbent services/distribution layer that blends source-statement data with no lineage, no ownership, no contract; its semantics differ from the CDM with no common abstraction layer between them. Risk apps also re-distribute their own computed outputs the same way — over services, no contract, FX from various sources, currency often undeclared.

The defect in one line: conformance is already happening — inside Veritas — but in the dark. The CDM doesn't start conformance; it moves conformance from an ungoverned service into a governed model.

01The risk landscape — a mesh, not a chain

Four product groups, many apps each. Apps consume from sources and from each other. The same entity (counterparty, book) is authored multiple times → divergent-conformance risk.

origin producer (non-risk) conformer (authors conformed entity) calculator (authors computed metric) internal consumer business-aligned (consumer) product

Input paths (heterogeneous)

A risk app's inputs may arrive three ways — and some apps do no conformance at all (direct-source, shaped ad hoc):

Source systems
trades · PETR (cpty) · market/ref → via Veritas (ungoverned)/ other services/ direct-to-source → risk apps

The apps & their roles

CRS — Credit Risk System conformer calculator

Ingests trades + PETR + reference data; shapes conformed entities (counterparty, book, credit hierarchy → clients_report, lines_report) and computes stress PFE / exposures (asts, ats_summary). The de-facto (undeclared) risk conformer.

Xvala conformer calculator

Consumes trades + sources directly, shapes them its own way, produces xval metrics; may also feed conformed-entity reports + metrics to risk business. → a second authority for "counterparty".

Market-risk apps consumer calculator

Consume conformed entities from CRS (book, credit hierarchy) as their trusted source; produce market-risk metrics. CRS output is upstream to them.

CMOS consumer calculator

Hybrid: consumes directly from sources and some conformed entities from CRS.

"Conformed entity" is a role a node plays, not a layer. CRS's counterparty is conformed-output to market risk but conformed-input shaped from PETR. The same node is producer on one edge and consumer on another — that is the mesh.
Today (maturity 0): each app emits CSV reports; business does cross-app conformance by hand in Excel, downstream of every app, with no model. Conformance happens at the worst possible place. The whole initiative = move conformance left.

02Story A — the producer / consumer product tree

What gets published, and how it flows. This story is about the supply chain.

The authorship test decides the producer boundary: not "is it raw," not "is it untouched" — who computed or shaped this fact? A heavily-shaped table is still a producer product if the domain authored the shaping.

ArchetypeDefinitionRisk examples
Producer · source-aligned
two tiers ↓		 The domain's authored output, as produced.
↳ conformed source (risk-shaped) CRS's authored conformance of borrowed raw inputs.clients_report, lines_report
↳ computed output (true risk-produced) Facts that exist nowhere upstream — computed by the engine.asts, ats_summary (stress PFE, scenario maxima, breach flags)
Consumer · business-aligned Purpose-built curation for a named audience.vw_asts_portfolio_cp_stress, vw_ast_breach_report (Stress-Test Forum)
Counter-intuitive but central: the more-derived ats_summary is a better producer candidate than the rawer-looking lines_report, because producer = authorship, not rawness. lines_report looks raw but is shaped-from-borrowed; ats_summary looks derived but is the engine's own computation.

What is not your producer product

The raw inputs — trades, PETR, market/reference data — are other domains' producers. The pfe_ prefix denotes the consuming process (CRS shaped them), not foreign ownership. Don't publish test_ tables (dev artifacts) or pfe_limit_config (a reference input, not a product) as products.

03Story C — the conformance ladder

Conformance composes upward; it can't skip. Each level uses the same metadata template at rising scope. representational = agree with yourself · semantic = agree with others · consumption.

L0

Within a table representational

dates→canonical · money-strings→numeric · FX declared · currency/unit stamped · grain + keys declared. = filling the template per table = the productization act itself. Profiler-automatable.
L1

Within an app internal

one app's tables agree (one counterparty, one calendar, one FX). Not a precondition for productizing a single dataset.
L2

Within a product group semantic

all credit-risk apps mean the same counterparty/book/rating; same for each group.
L3

Across product groups semantic = the Risk CDM

credit / market / L&F / finance agree on shared entities. "Risk CDM = what the templates agree on at L3."
L4

Risk Analytics consumption

works off the conformed risk domain, not off CSVs. Replaces manual cross-app reconciliation. Strategy / Mosaic lives here — consumes conformed, never performs conformance.
L5

CDO CDM enterprise

risk binds to enterprise-canonical entities. Fan-out, later.
Most programs fail by attempting L2/L3 before L0/L1. You can't conform across apps while individual tables still ship comma-strings and undeclared currency. Bottom-up is mandatory — but overlap groups rather than serialise (start binding shared keys as soon as two groups reach L1).

04Story B — Risk CDM vs CDO CDM

What the entities are (the nouns, conformed). This story is about semantics. Stories A and B are the two halves of governance: supply chain + semantics.

CDO CDM enterprise · authoritative

The firm's single authoritative definition of shared entities — what a counterparty is, its identifier (LEI), canonical attributes. Owned by CDO / reference data, application-agnostic. New governed artifact. Answers: "what is this entity, enterprise-wide?"

Risk CDM domain · risk-authored aspirational today

The risk domain's conformed model — entities shaped for risk: counterparty + credit hierarchy, worst-rating roll-up, book, netting set, line structure — concepts the CDO model doesn't carry. Consistent across risk apps. Answers: "what is this entity, for risk, consistently across our apps?"
The relationship (one sentence for the explainer): the CDO CDM defines the entity for the enterprise; the Risk CDM defines the risk-domain's conformed extension of that entity. The Risk CDM reconciles to the CDO CDM for shared identity and adds the risk-specific structure — it must extend, not fork.

Status today: the Risk CDM does not exist as a governed artifact — but CRS already de-facto conforms and other risk apps consume some of it. So: "you already have a Risk CDM — it's CRS's conformance, undeclared. The task is to formalise it, not invent it." The enterprise counterparty key exists → binding is real, so divergent-conformance is fixable, not structural.

Failure mode — "Veritas 2.0": if risk conforms internally without binding to the enterprise key from day one, it builds a risk model that competes with the enterprise entity — ungoverned relative to CDO, the same no-common-abstraction problem one level up.

05Decisions & rules captured

RuleWhy
Productization ≠ conformance — independent tracks. Products never wait on conformance.A dataset can be owned + contracted + semantically declared while still unconformed across (or within) apps. The gap is declared conformance debt in the contract, not a blocker.
The unit of productization is the single dataset — not the app.Even datasets within one app needn't conform to each other for any one of them to publish. Smallest possible unit ⇒ nothing is ever blocked.
L0 (fill the template) IS productization — and the prerequisite for all higher conformance.Productize-now isn't a detour from conformance; it lays its groundwork. Value now, conformance compounds.
Bind the enterprise key AT publish — the only non-deferrable step.Bind low ⇒ later conformance is verification, not rework. Skip it ⇒ re-open every product to conform.
Extend, not fork the CDO entity.Avoids Veritas 2.0 — a risk model competing with the enterprise definition.
Declared non-conformance > invisible non-conformance.The gap exists either way; only a governed product tells you. Contract makes the debt visible, owned metadata.
Conformance happens in governed layers, never in the BI tool.Conforming inside Strategy/Mosaic = the Excel problem one tool up — ungoverned, lineage-invisible, unowned.
The metadata template is the abstraction layer between apps/Veritas and the CDM.Capturing producer domain, conformed entity, bound key, FX source, currency, unit per product makes "map to CDO CDM" a fill-in-the-fields exercise, not a re-architecture.
The canonical teaching example — FX/currency: two risk apps compute "exposure" using FX rates from different sources, neither declaring currency or FX source, both distributing via contractless services. Downstream, someone sums them. The numbers are incomparable and nothing flags it. One bug = missing semantics (currency/unit) + missing supply-chain metadata (FX source) + missing contract + ungoverned distribution. The template's Reporting currency, FX source, FX rate date, Measure unit fields were designed to prevent exactly this.

07Glossary — working vocabulary

Source systems
External systems of record CRS/apps ingest from: trade/booking, PETR (counterparty), market/reference data. Other domains' producers.
CRS (Credit Risk System)
The risk producing application; ingests sources, conforms entities, and computes exposure. The de-facto (undeclared) risk conformer.
Conformed source data (risk-shaped)
CRS's authored conformance of borrowed raw inputs — clients_report, lines_report. Producer-tier, "shaped input".
Risk-computed data (true risk-produced)
Facts computed by the engine, existing nowhere upstream — asts, ats_summary. Producer-tier, "computed output".
Producer / source-aligned data product
Governed publication of a domain's authored output (conformed or computed).
Consumer / business-aligned data product
Purpose-built curation for a named audience (the Forum views).
Authorship test
The rule for the producer boundary: who computed/shaped this fact? Not rawness, not untouched-ness.
Veritas
Incumbent ungoverned distribution/services layer; blends source-statement data; no lineage/ownership/contract; semantics differ from CDM with no common abstraction.
Two distribution planes
Governed (UC/CDM/lineage/contracts) vs ungoverned (Veritas + contractless app services). The same fact can travel both.
Ungoverned conformance
Conformance/blending happening inside Veritas with no model/owner/lineage — the thing the CDM brings into the light.
Contractless distribution
Apps shipping computed outputs over services with no contract and undeclared currency/FX — governance lost at the doorstep.
CDO CDM
New governed, enterprise-canonical conformed data model; one authoritative entity definition (LEI-keyed); owned by CDO/reference data.
Risk CDM
Aspirational risk-domain conformed model extending the CDO entity with risk concepts (credit hierarchy, netting, worst-rating, line structure); consistent across risk apps.
Extend, not fork
The Risk CDM binds to the CDO entity for identity and adds risk structure — it must not re-author the enterprise entity.
Conformance ladder L0–L5
representational (table) → internal (app) → cross-app (group) → cross-group (Risk CDM) → consumption (analytics) → enterprise (CDO).
Representational vs semantic conformance
Agree-with-yourself (L0/L1, mechanical, profiler-automatable) vs agree-with-others (L2/L3, needs Risk CDM + shared keys).
Moving conformance left
From the consumer's spreadsheet (today, L4-by-hand) into governed layers, earliest-possible.
Declared conformance debt
Within/cross-app disagreement recorded in a product's contract — visible, owned metadata rather than an invisible landmine.
The template as conformance instrument
One artifact applied at rising scope: fill at L0, agree-check at L1/L2/L3.
Risk Analytics (new)
L4 capability that works off the conformed risk domain, replacing manual cross-app CSV reconciliation.
